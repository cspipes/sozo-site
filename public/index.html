Security

Fun to use. Serious about security.

Your clients trust you with their homes, their businesses and their health information. Sozo protects all of it with proven, industry-standard technology, built in from the first line of code.

Encrypted everywhere

TLS 1.2 or higher in transit and AES-256 at rest, the same standard banks rely on. Social Security numbers get a second, separate layer of encryption on top.

Enterprise-grade cloud

Sozo runs on a leading enterprise cloud. Your data and documents stay off the public internet, and every key and password is kept in a dedicated secrets vault, never in the code.

Secure sign-in

Your team signs in with their existing work accounts, protected by multi-factor authentication. No new passwords to create, reuse or forget.

Walls the database enforces

Access is role-based and closed by default. Health information is walled off inside the database itself, not just hidden on a screen, and it never leaves in a plain email.

A record of everything

Append-only audit logs show who opened health information, what changed, and what anyone asked the AI. Nothing can quietly rewrite history.

Your own install, backed up nightly

Each agency runs on its own instance and its own database, never mixed with anyone else's book, with nightly backups and point-in-time restore.

Questions

Sozo, in plain English.

What is Sozo?

Sozo is AI-powered agency management software (an AMS) for independent insurance agencies. It reads the dec pages, quotes, endorsements and statements you already receive and does the data entry for you, across commercial lines, personal lines and employee benefits.

Who built Sozo?

Sozo was built inside a working independent insurance agency, by people who handle renewals, service and commissions every day. It's agency software designed by agents, for agents.

How does Sozo use AI?

Sozo uses AI to read your documents and fill in policies, coverages, limits and forms, and to answer questions about your book in plain English through Ask Sozo. Anything it isn't sure about is flagged for a person to review, so your team stays in charge.

How is Sozo different from other agency management systems?

Most systems expect your team to key in what the carrier already sent, and to work the way the software was built. Sozo starts from the document and does the typing, and it adapts to your agency: your stages, timing and tasks are settings your managers shape, not code. Your agency also owns its data outright and can export all of it, anytime, free.

Who is Sozo for?

Independent insurance agencies that write property and casualty, employee benefits or both, and want to spend less time on data entry and more time with clients.
