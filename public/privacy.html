Privacy

Last updated October 21, 2026. This page covers sozoware.com. It's short because we keep very little.

What we collect

When you join the pilot list, we collect what you type into the form: your name, your agency, your work email and, if you choose, your team size. We use it to contact you about the Sozo pilot program and nothing else.

Site analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't follow you to other sites and doesn't identify you. That's why this site has no cookie banner.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your information, we don't use cookies or advertising trackers on this site, and we don't add you to a mailing list you didn't ask for.

Services this site uses

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs and may use a spam check (Cloudflare Turnstile) on the form. Our fonts load from Google Fonts. Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

Want us to update or delete what you've sent us? Email hello@sozoware.com and we'll take care of it.
